# F1 Strategy Reference

## Pit Strategy

### Undercut
Pitting **sooner than your opponent**.

- Get onto fresh tyres earlier.
- Try to gain time while the opponent is still on older tyres.
- You need enough pace after the pit stop to make the strategy work.
- Track position and traffic are important.

### Overcut
Pitting **later than your opponent**.

- Stay out while your opponent pits.
- Can work if you have good pace on the current tyres.
- Clean air can help.
- Sometimes you may have a different strategy/tyre plan.

### Stint
A **period of time/laps between two pit stops**, usually spent on the same set of tyres.

Example:

`Start → Medium → Pit Stop → Hard → Finish`

This gives you **2 stints**.
